# Saturday, 2026-08-15

## Objective

Complete the medium-scale A100 evaluation for the matched BC, Top-K, and
CAT-K SMART-mini checkpoints.

## Completed Runs

- Data: 19,611 training and 1,151 validation scenarios, seed 817.
- Hardware: one NVIDIA A100-PCIE-40GB, 12 CPU cores, 90 GiB RAM.
- BC: 8 epochs, batch size 4, FP16 mixed precision.
- Top-K and CAT-K: 4 epochs, batch size 4, BF16 mixed precision.
- Validation: 4 rollouts/20 batches, 8 rollouts/50 batches, and WOSAC-20
  with 32 rollouts.

## Failure And Resolution

The first FP16 Top-K run failed at epoch 0, batch 149. Closed-loop inference
produced NaN logits for `Categorical` sampling in a batch containing 227
agents. Host memory, disk, and GPU memory were healthy. Both methods passed a
250-batch BF16 stress test, then completed their full runs in 5 h 33 min and
5 h 36 min respectively.

## Result

CAT-K reduced 8-rollout/50-batch closed-loop ADE from BC's 0.44384 to
0.42888. On WOSAC-20 it reduced internal ADE from 0.27328 to 0.25530, while
realism was effectively tied at 0.71113 versus 0.71116. Top-K degraded
substantially. Full tables and interpretation are in `medium_20k_results.md`.

## Decision

Treat the medium-scale qualitative result as reproduced: CAT-K improves the
internal closed-loop displacement metric and Top-K is unstable as a training
strategy. Do not claim that CAT-K wins every WOSAC bucket from only 20
scenarios. Preserve the three checkpoints externally, commit aggregate
results only, and use a larger fixed WOSAC prefix only if rental time permits.
